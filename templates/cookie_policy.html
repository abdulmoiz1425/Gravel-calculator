This Cookie Policy explains how GravelYardCalculator.com (gravelyardcalculator.com) uses cookies and similar technologies. It supplements our Privacy Policy.
1

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website asks your browser to store. Cookies set by the website you are visiting are first-party cookies. Cookies set by other companies are third-party cookies. Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser, while persistent cookies stay until they expire or you delete them.

2

Cookies This Website Sets

Essential cookie. GravelYardCalculator.com sets one essential cookie, and only when you open the Contact page:

  • Name: csrftoken
  • Purpose: Security. It protects the contact form against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).
  • Type: First-party, essential
  • Duration: Up to one year
  • Content: A random security token. It contains no personal information and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Analytics cookies (only after you press Accept). If you accept, Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies:

  • _ga: Distinguishes visitors so usage can be measured. Google documents a lifetime of up to two years.
  • _ga_KVPEYSQ98D: Keeps session state for our Google Analytics property. Google documents a lifetime of up to two years.

Your consent choice. The choice you make on the banner is saved in your browser's local storage under the name gyc_analytics_consent. It is not a cookie and is not sent to the server.

If you deny or make no choice, no other cookies are set when you use the calculators, the blog, or the other pages.

3

Cookies and Tools We Do Not Use

The website does not currently use advertising cookies, affiliate tracking cookies, or social media embeds, and it does not store your calculator inputs in cookies or browser storage. Analytics cookies are used only if you accept them on the cookie banner. If we add any other services, we will update this policy and the Privacy Policy to describe them.

4

Third-Party Resources

If you accept analytics, pages also load Google Analytics from Google (googletagmanager.com and google-analytics.com). Google receives your IP address and the pages you visit. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Every page loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When your browser loads them, it sends standard request information, such as your IP address, to Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The website links to other sites, such as social media profiles and blog share buttons. Those sites may set their own cookies once you visit them, and we do not control them.

5

Your Cookie Choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the security cookie described above may stop the contact form from working.

Google Chrome Settings → Privacy → Cookies
Mozilla Firefox Options → Privacy & Security
Apple Safari Preferences → Privacy
Microsoft Edge Settings → Cookies & Site Permissions
Opera Browser Settings → Advanced → Privacy
Brave Browser Settings → Shields → Cookies

You can change your analytics choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. Pressing Deny stops Google Analytics from loading and removes the analytics cookies this website can reach. Google also offers a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics.

6

Do Not Track

The website does not track visitors, so a "Do Not Track" browser signal does not change what the website does.

7

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We update this policy when the cookies or third-party resources used by the website change. The "Last Updated" date shows the latest revision.
