This Privacy Policy explains how GravelYardCalculator.com (gravelyardcalculator.com) handles information, based on how the website works today. By using the website you agree to the practices described in this policy.
1

Information We Collect

Information You Provide to Us

If you send a message through the Contact page, you provide your name, email address, a subject, and your message. The contact form stores these details in the website's database, where site administrators can read them so we can review and respond. Messages stay in the database until they are deleted.

Information Collected Automatically

The website does not run analytics or tracking tools, and it does not collect information about your device or browsing activity itself.

2

Calculator Information

Your calculator inputs are processed entirely in your browser. The calculators do not send the values you enter to our servers, and the website does not store them.

The dimensions, materials, prices, and other inputs you enter into the gravel calculators are processed within your web browser using JavaScript. The website does not transmit or store these values, and it does not save them in cookies or browser storage.

3

How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Read and respond to messages sent through the Contact page
  • Correct calculation errors and improve the calculators and guides, based on feedback you send us
  • Operate, maintain, and secure the website
  • Comply with legal obligations
4

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website itself sets one cookie, and only on the Contact page: a security cookie named csrftoken. It protects the contact form against cross-site request forgery, lasts up to one year, and is not used for tracking or advertising. No analytics, advertising, or affiliate cookies are set. See our Cookie Policy for details.

5

Third-Party Services and Links

Google Fonts

Pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), so Google receives your IP address and standard request information. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, Advertising, and Affiliate Links

The website does not currently use analytics, advertising, or affiliate services. If that changes, this policy and the Cookie Policy will be updated to describe it.

Links to Other Websites

The website links to other sites, such as social media profiles and share buttons on blog posts. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their policies.

6

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. Messages sent through the Contact page are not shared with advertisers or analytics providers, because none are used. They are stored on the server that hosts the website, so the hosting provider can technically access them, and we may disclose information if required by law or legal process.

7

Data Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Calculator inputs stay in your browser. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your messages.

8

Your Choices

  • Cookies: You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. See the Cookie Policy.
  • Google Fonts: You can block requests to Google's font servers with a browser setting or extension. The site will then show a default font.
  • Your messages: To ask about, correct, or delete a message you sent, use the Contact page and include the name and email you used.
9

Children's Privacy

GravelYardCalculator.com is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10

Changes to This Policy

We update this policy when the website's practices change, for example if analytics, advertising, or other services are added. The "Last Updated" date shows the latest revision.

11

Contact Us

Privacy Questions?

If you have a question about this policy or a request about a message you sent, please use our Contact page.
